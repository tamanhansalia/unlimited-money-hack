# Jobs page plan

1. Give Jobs a dedicated `#/jobs` route and sidebar destination. Open existing `#/generate/jobs` links on the Jobs page as well.
2. Use Generate for video setup and preview. After a valid submission, open its job at the top of the list. Pending Library cards and active Autopilot profiles link to their own job.
3. Show the current session’s manual and Autopilot jobs with All jobs, Active, and History filters. Each row shows its source, status, live progress or result, and any engine output. Manual jobs offer Cancel, Stop, and Use settings again. Autopilot manages its own runs. View clips is available while the job has clips in the Library.
4. Verify the sidebar and legacy route, empty and filtered job lists, submission, queue progress, and job actions. Confirm the Jobs page fits narrow windows and Generate still restores a job’s settings.
